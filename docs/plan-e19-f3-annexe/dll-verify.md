## 2. Corrections and additions

### 2.1 The binary's callers of the opener (the plan's "three unidentified callers"), read now
[bin] `jal 0x80050BA8` appears at **6** addresses, and the twin `jal 0x80050C00` at 1 (`jal.py`):
| caller | ETC labels (`GetEtcString` before the call) | counterpart |
|---|---|---|
| 0x8003E8E4 | 0x43/0x44 | opcode 0x44 (ported) |
| 0x80059504 | 0x4A/0x4B | save carousel question (ported) |
| 0x8007BA54 | 0x41/0x42 | save book (ported) |
| 0x8005FC68 | 0x81/0x82 (after 0x93/0x94 "Impossible d'enregistrer l'histoire / Est-ce bien clair?") | `MemoryCardManager.cs:895`, not ported |
| 0x8005F9D0 (twin 0x80050C00; sets the result slot 0x800C4988 to 2 first; state 0x69) | 0x81/0x82 | `MemoryCardManager.cs:743`, not ported (the report said the twin had no caller) |
| 0x8005EDA8 | literal Japanese strings at 0x80026C18/0x80026C20 | `MemoryCardManager.cs:584` (state 0x2710, debug), not ported |
| 0x80060DF8 inside a wrapper at 0x80060D8C | caller-supplied | **dead**: no `jal`, no `j`, no data pointer, no lui/addiu to 0x80060D8C in the whole executable; the decompilation has no counterpart |
So the "three unidentified callers" are: ETC 0x81/0x82 (two memory-card flows), a debug literal, a dead wrapper. None is a DLL feature; the DLL's card-failure path is a message then Square. The ETC table also holds a fifth OUI/NON pair, ETC 0xCA/0xCB (offsets 5541/5545, question 0xC9 "Veux-tu charger cette histoire?"), which the report omits: no `GetEtcString` call with that literal exists in the executable (`etcuse.py`) nor in the decompilation, so it is unused here.

### 2.2 Opener facts that bear on f3 (read now, [bin])
- 0x80050BA8: stores the two string pointers and the result pointer, `jal 0x800505FC`, clears the countdown global at 0x8017E670 (`lui 0x8018; sw zero,-0x1990`, `g_asyncOperationCountdown`), returns 1. The twin 0x80050C00 sets that countdown to `*result - 1` and zeroes the result (callers preset 2, so countdown 1).
- 0x800505FC copies each label with `strncpy(dst, src, 6)` (0x8008202C, n = 6): **labels are cut at 6 bytes**; then `jal 0x800506FC` (initialises the choice box), `PlaySoundEffect(4)` (0x800490FC), `SetTransitionType(3)`. The DLL bounds the carousel's labels at 0x40 (`MaxLineLength`); with OUI/NON the difference is invisible, but a faithful machine should hold labels of at most 6 characters (D-E19-81).
- The callback 0x80050B98 is just `*resultPtr = a0`.

### 2.3 Map 143 `0x44 @751` is a subroutine, and the report's detail row for it is a heuristic artefact
[calc] `census.py` assigned it to "C[9], open `0D@730`, node `M143_S006`" because it takes the last open before the site in linear order. The code at 740-754 is a shared question subroutine called by `0x78` from four programs (C[10] @940, C[11] @1224, C[12] @1508, C[13] @1792 of map 143), each with its own `0x5C` open just before the call (`cfg.py`: 4 openers reach the site backward, all control mode 1). The report's 101 is right; its node attribution for that row and the "86 distinct questions" (95 with the CFG rule, which also counts loop-back opens) are heuristic. Not material.

## 3. What the report missed that could change the plan

1. **The memo hypothesis is gone** (item 4): do not design a "retry while another async operation runs" behaviour; the binary has none. A new machine may keep one global slot (as the binary does: one callback pointer, one pair of label buffers) and refuse/overwrite on a second opener, but no corpus site needs it.
2. **9 sites do not tie the choice to the end of the typing** (item 14): an f3 machine must not assume the text box has finished (or even been drawn) when the choice opens; the K5 test (`AlundraDialogueBoxOrderTests.cs:324`, `OpenChoice` at frame 30 while the box is typing) is the right shape for them. With A (separate screens) that costs nothing.
3. **The twin opener and the two ETC 0x81/0x82 flows exist in the binary** (2.1): if the f3 hook is meant to cover "every caller of the binary's opener", the 23/24 sites are only the DLL's three; the other four live paths are memory-card flows the DLL does not port. Say so in the plan so the hook is not specified as universal.
4. **Label truncation at 6 characters** and the **sound 4 played by the opener itself, not by the box** (2.2) belong in the f3 contract (D-E19-81).
5. The plan figure "23 sites" can be corrected to 24 in the plan, and "117 sites in 52 maps" of `plan-e12-dialogues.md:109` to 101 in 39 (the plan's E12 line is an audit doc: record in an ADR/annex, do not edit the audit).
6. The report's open question 1 (when the result is delivered) is still open and is the single decision that moves the 24 test sites; nothing in the DLL surface settles it. The opener returns immediately (it only arms the callback): the delivery is in the update 0x800501FC, which is the binary surface's to read.
