# ADR-0066: The shared random stream steps once per logic tick, through an optional per-tick callback of the effect stage

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-10-15
- **Source**: this chantier: `docs/plan-e20-heros.md`, section 1.17 (E20.e0b, D-E0B-1, D-E0B-2, D-E0B-3, D-E0B-7, D-E0B-8) and 1.17.1 (E20.e0b1, revisions 1 and 2, READY at its extra closing review); evidence in `docs/plan-e20-annexe/e20-e0b/` (`oracle/`: the frame-function probe of `ALUN_CD.EXE`, France, `0x8002BAEC`, ten rows and twenty one-word mutants; `e0b1-shadow.txt`: the shadow run of the whole suite); the author's answers Q-B1, Q-B2, Q-B4 of 2026-10-15

## Context

- The binary's frame function (`0x8002BAEC`) ends with one step of the shared generator, `s <- s * 0x7D2B89DD + 0xE06A02E7 (mod 2^32)` (`0x8002BD0C-0x8002BD34`), the result never read, on every path of the function: the menu gate `[0x800DC4B8] & 0x48`, a refused menu, `a0 = 0` or `1` change nothing (only the passes of the lists are skipped under the gate). The function is called once after each map load (`a0 = 1`) and once per turn of the main loop (`a0 = 0`); the transition loop (`0x8002C490-0x8002C4C0`) calls neither it nor the advance. The six words of the oracle are `0 -> E06A02E7`, `FFFFFFFF -> 633E790A`, `80000000 -> 606A02E7`, `B017C93D -> 35E36190`, `12345678 -> B806E07F`, four steps `-> AFBE9EAC`.
- The DLL never advanced the stream per frame: `AlundraRandom` had `Next()` and `Reset()` only, and a comment of the world said the generator was "advanced per frame". The draws of the DLL are of two kinds: immediate ones (the random opcodes, the AI of the Beannoids and of the Muruta) and ones deferred into `AlundraEffectStage.RunFrame` (the sparks of charge, of hit and of the vessel). A logic tick of the DLL is not a rendered frame: a frame holds 0 to 4 ticks, and the effect stage runs once per frame over all its ticks.
- Placing the advance after `RunFrame`, or unconditionally inside it, was refuted by the discovery: the first breaks the invariance to the grouping of ticks (the spark of the tick 89 falls on the second tick of the frame 88-90 and does not see the advance of tick 88), the second breaks the direct calls of the stage that expect an untouched stream. A pass name added to the loop of the ticks breaks the tick-structure test.
- The measuring was done in two phases: a neutral shadow first (the callback wired, the advance writing only a virtual word `V` next to the real state `R`, every would-be divergent draw or seed read logged per test with the outermost `Alundra.Tests` frame of the stack), then the behaviour. The whole suite with the shadow on (4 834 tests, 147 145 virtual advances) predicted 66 tests whose reads of the stream would differ (`divergent_draws + divergent_seed_reads + ambient_draws > 0`).

## Decision

- **A per-tick callback.** `AlundraEffectStage.RunFrame(int ticks, IReadOnlyList<bool> frozenOfTick, Action? afterTick = null)` calls `afterTick` once per tick, after the update of the reservoir, whether the tick is frozen or not, never for 0 ticks. It is `null` by default, so the direct calls of the stage (70 in the tests) advance nothing. The world passes a static delegate (`AdvanceRandomPerTick`, no allocation per tick) that calls `AlundraRandom.AdvanceFrame()`.
- **`AlundraRandom.AdvanceFrame()`** applies the same `ulong` expression as `Next()` to the real state and returns nothing. `RandomSeed` becomes a property (source-compatible: nothing passes it by `ref`) so that the sink sees the assignments and the reads; `Next()` works on the private field alone.
- **When it advances.** Once per logic tick, also under the menu gate `0x48`, in the menus, the book and the inventory (the binary does; default faithful, author's answer Q-B4). Not for a frame without tick, not for a frame that departs to a warp (`RunFrame` receives 0 ticks while a transition is in progress). The first frame of a world counts at least one tick (the existing floor of `LogicTicksThisFrame`), which stands for the binary's `a0 = 1` call after the load.
- **Documented deviations (accepted, Q-B2).** In a frame of several ticks the immediate draws of the later ticks (AI, `0x0C`, `0x8C`) do not see the intermediate advances while the draws of the effects do (exact interleaving); the tick that raises a warp does not advance (the binary does: one advance less per warp); the first frame of a world may hold up to 4 ticks after a long loading pause (inference; the binary: one, `a0 = 1`). No placement is exact in all three cases without moving the effect pass into the loop of the ticks, which is out of the slice.
- **The measuring sink stays in production code** (`AlundraRandomShadow`), inert without `ALUNDRA_RNG_SHADOW_LOG` (no file, no allocation, no counter touched; guarded by a test), like the slot-table channel (ADR-0064). After the phase B it keeps `V = R`.
- **Every moved pin is rewritten by a derivation** (D-E0B-8): the expected word is computed by a local step from the seed and the counts of ticks and draws, never a pasted constant. This applies to the new tests; no existing pin needed rewriting (see Consequences).

## Consequences

- The stream the sparks, the AI and the opcodes see is now the stream of the binary's frame loop, up to the three deviations above. This is the prerequisite of the hit sparks of E20.f3 and of the loot roll of E20.e0b2 (which is measured on a code that already advances).
- Tests: `AlundraRandomPerTickTests` (18 cases): (a) the six words of the binary by `AdvanceFrame()`; (b) the callback once per tick, after the reservoir tick, also on a frozen tick, never for 0 ticks, after the queued operations and the update of its tick; (c) at the world level, 1 tick = 1 step, 2 ticks = 2, the gate `0x48` = 1 per tick, the first frame = 1, two successive worlds without a double count, a frame without tick then one tick = exactly 1 step in all, a frame that departs to a warp between two frames of one tick = exactly 2 steps in all; (d) the charge sparks of the ticks 83, 87 and 91 of the steel-sword arena draw before the advance of their tick, identically in frames of 1, 2 and 3 ticks; (e) `RunFrame` without callback advances nothing. `Alundra.Tests` goes from 4 833 to 4 852 in Release and in Debug.
- **No existing test moved**, although the shadow predicted 66 (the draws differ but the divergence reaches no assertion; the reasons by family are in `docs/plan-e20-annexe/e20-e0b/README.md`). The prediction of the discovery that `A5r` would move was not borne out: the direction pinned after the frame 120 survives by coincidence, as the discovery had noted for 219 to 221 advances (note 6 of 1.17). A future change that makes a draw reach an assertion will be caught by those tests, not by this ADR.
- Known limits, deferred: the loot roll at creation (E20.e0b2); the transition effects 4, 5 and 6, which draw from the same stream and that the DLL treats as effect 0 (O-E0B-1); the re-rolls of `0x8003A048` and `0x8003ABCC` (E14); moving the effect pass into the loop of the ticks would remove the three deviations.
