# ADR-0109: The earth tome 2 is driven by the table driver 0x800354D0 and its rocks by E13, registered for the sprite type 17 alone with a ground guard, and the impact kicks the camera

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-10-28
- **Source**: this chantier: `docs/plan-e20-heros.md` section 1.31.4 (E20.h4c2, revision 3.2; D-H4C2-1 to D-H4C2-5; D-E20-56 (Q-H4C2-3, B); defaults of Q-H4B-4, Q-H4C2-1, Q-H4C2-2, Q-H4C2-5 (A), Q-H4C2-6 (A)); `docs/plan-e20-annexe/e20-h4c2/` (`t0/results.md`, `t1/results.md`, `t2/results.md`, `t3/results.md`, `rockfix/`); `docs/plan-e20-annexe/e20-h4/` and `e20-h4b/` (the reading of the binary, `vec_e13`, `vec_camera`); ADR-0107 (the earth tome 1, the model of this slice)

## Context

- The item `0x2C` (the earth tome 2) is driven in the executable by the function `0x800354D0` and a table of 15 entries at `0x80023504`; the rocks it throws (sprite type 17) run the native handler E13 (`0x8007F30C`, slot E) once they stop, and E13 assigns the camera kick byte `[0x800DD874]` that the camera step (`0x8002CDA0`) adds to the scroll Y. Until E20.h4c2 the DLL had the start, the states and the slope guard of the tomes (ADR-0105) and the stubs of the three item seams for `0x2C`.
- The decompilation was not the oracle: the executable (France) was read and its code replayed by an index-writing machine (604 driver vectors, 40 vectors of E13, 100 vectors of the camera step), then the keys of the original run were stored.
- The original plays the impact (the animation, the sound 45 and the camera kick) as soon as a rock is stopped, even in the air: a rock that a wall or the edge of the map stops in mid-fall explodes in the air. The author decided that this is a defect to correct (D-E20-56, answer B of Q-H4C2-3), and took the default of Q-H4C2-5 (A: the rock keeps pushing and slides along the wall or the edge, then lands) and Q-H4C2-6 (A: the earth tome 1 and the fire balls, which run the same code, keep the original).
- The sprite birth by the interface kept the sub-pixel fraction only after E20.e0a6 (ADR-0103), and the probe (s2) of T0 found x and y without the fraction for 13 of 15 births; D-H4C2-5 made T1 replay (s2) first (x and y equal to the key), which held. The coup in X (`[0x800DD870]`) has no member in the DLL and no ported code writes it; 22 of the 100 vectors of the camera step that read it are classed "binary only" and skipped (D-H4C2-5, O-H4C2-1).

## Decision

- A new file `Alundra/Ai/Items/EarthTome2Ai.cs` holds the 64 bytes of the table (15 entries of `u16` tick, `s16` p, and the sentinel `0xFFFF`), the driver, the cancellation `0x80034BDC` and E13 (D-H4C2-1).
- The driver: phase 0 (slope guard, then for each entry whose tick is the counter two draws of the generator, `r1` then `r2`, below 32, BEFORE the birth and whatever its result, then a rock born by `SpawnEntityBySpriteIndex` at `hero + (X32[r1], Y32[r1]) * (p + 0xA00)`, direction `r2`, at the Z of the hero plus 256 px; a newborn whose terrain is strictly above `heroPosZ + 1 + 0x1000000` is destroyed on the spot, any other gets the Z force of -8 px a tick; the phase is 1 once the 15 entries have passed); phase 1 (guard, then at the counter `0x118` the animation `0x34` and the phase 2); phase 2 without guard (0 while the animation is `0x34`, else 1). The hero is not moved.
- E13 is registered by `EarthTome2Ai.Register` for the sprite type 17 alone (not 469, not 16) and is part of `NativeSpriteRegistry.CreateForProduction`. When the animation asked is 1 it destroys the rock (`DestroyEntity(rock, -1)`) at the end of the animation and writes nothing else. Otherwise, and only if the rock is on the ground (**deviation from the binary, D-E20-56 and D-H4C2-3**), it asks the animation 1, sets the flags to `(flags | 0x40) & ~0x30`, resyncs the controller and assigns the camera kick 2. A rock stopped in the air has its status put back to 2 and nothing else written; the guard reads `IsOnGround`, never the wall flag or `ForceAdjusted`, so every cause of a stop in the air is covered, and it is on the first branch alone (a cancelled rock, already in the animation 1, waits for the end of its animation on the ground or not).
- The cancellation `0x80034BDC` sends every entity of the type 17 at the status 2 to the status 3, clears its blocker and asks the animation 1; nothing else is written. The three entries `0x2C` of the item seams (start = the tome start of h4a, driver, cancellation) of `PlayerManager` are wired in the same slice.
- The camera kick (D-H4C2-2): `IEntityWorldContext.SetCameraKick(int)` is a default interface member that does nothing; `WorldProxy` files the kick by logic tick of the frame (assigned, not added), at the index set at the head of the entity block (0 outside it), and empties the list AFTER the camera step of the frame. `CameraDirector.UpdateCameraFollow` hands it to `CameraMath.AdvanceCameraSmoothing` (a final optional parameter), which adds it to the sway Y offset at each follow step: the kick of a snap tick is lost and none is applied during a warp transition.
- The deviation applies to the type 17 only: E3 (type 16) and the fire balls keep the original behaviour in the air (Q-H4C2-6, A).

## Consequences

- The earth tome 2 works in the game: 15 rocks along the table, the sound 55 at the births and 45 at the impacts, the camera kick of 2 at each impact, the object freed at the tick 309; the cancellation stops the rocks in the air and plays the sound 45 for each. A rock held in the air by a wall or the edge slides, lands and then explodes (the position at the end is 39 px farther than the binary's in the witness scene of the edge, which a measured key supports; the keys of the deviation are marked "indicative, not faithful to the byte" in the annex).
- The five sums of the keys of the production registry are derived from `EarthTome2Ai.Keys`, not retyped. The camera step with no kick list is the step of before the slice (all the existing calls).
- Tests: 7785 in the suite after T3, Release and Debug without failure (T2: 7784, plus the survivor test of T3). 46 real mutants of the slice were applied by script and undone (`docs/plan-e20-annexe/e20-h4c2/t3/mutants.py`, results in `t3/results.md`), all killed; a 47th did not compile and was dropped. The declared equivalent (the controller resync in E13) survives and is proven equivalent by reading (the mask reads the classes A and B alone and the gravity setting is constant). One mutant survived at first (the reset of the tick index of the kick after the entity block) and got a test.
- Deferred: the other elements of the tomes, the coup in X of the camera (O-H4C2-1), E13 for the type 469, and the loot.
