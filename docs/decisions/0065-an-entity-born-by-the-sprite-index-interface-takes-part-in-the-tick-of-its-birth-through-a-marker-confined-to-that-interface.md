# ADR-0065: An entity born by the sprite-index interface takes part in the tick of its birth, through a marker confined to that interface

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-10-15
- **Source**: this chantier: `docs/plan-e20-heros.md`, section 1.16.3b (E20.e0a3b, revision 3, READY at its closing review; "Résultat de T0" and "Résultat de T0′"); oracle `docs/plan-e20-annexe/e20-e0a/oracle/out/v6.json` (rows `BT-M1` to `BT-M3`, `BT-H1` to `BT-H6`, `BT-L1` to `BT-L3`); decisions D-E0A-5, D-E0A-8, D-E20-34, D-E20-35; ADR-0063 and ADR-0064 (the interface and the slot table this builds on)

## Context

- In the binary, an entity created inside `MovePlayer` (the shot of the hero, the use of an item) is processed by the passes of the very tick that sees it born: its program A runs once, `+0x1C` becomes 1, it is in the lists, and the animation clock and the physics see it (oracle V6, `BT-M1` to `BT-M3`). One created in the native program of another entity is reported by one tick (`BT-H2`, `BT-H3`, `BT-H5`), or runs A twice (`BT-H1`, `BT-H4`, `BT-H6`, the gap D-E20-34, not ported).
- The DLL ticks an entity only once the engine has integrated it, at the head of the next frame (`TookPartInFrame`), and a spawned entity has no `Owner.World` before that, so every read of the world's collision field returns nothing. The engine adds a `Move` without a test before the integration (probe P-A, lines `CharacterControllerComponent.cs:1050-1056`) and the sprite has no animation yet (D-E20-35: it is drawn from the next frame, no engine change).
- The first plan of the code (`Current = Target` at creation, from P-A) was stopped by T0: for a downward entity (direction 0) it leaves no switch pending, so `AnimFlags` stays 0 forever and the appearance flag never falls.

## Decision

- **A marker, `JoinsBirthTick`**, on the proxy, posed ONLY by `AlundraWorldProxy.SpawnEntityBySpriteIndex`, as its last action, after the push of the position to the root (so nothing of the creation reads the collision field by the fallback); never by the record path, never cloned. The world counts the interface's births of the tick (reset at the head of the entity block) and, when the count is not zero right after `MovePlayer`, collects the tick's entities again. `CollectTickEntities` keeps `TookPartInFrame || (JoinsBirthTick && Owner.World == null)`. An entity born later in the tick (the programs of the others) is collected from the next tick, as the oracle's reported rows (D-E20-34, pinned by a test named after the gap).
- **The collision field of the host stands in for the world's one, for a marked entity not yet integrated**: `IAlundraScriptHost.CollisionField` (default member returning null, implemented explicitly by `AlundraWorldProxy` because its own property has another type), read through `AlundraEntityScriptProxy.ReadCollisionField()` by the nine former reads of `Owner.World.CollisionField`. An entity from a record keeps the exact read it had.
- **D-E0A-8: no `Current = Target`.** In `CreateEntityFromSpriteIndex` only (never in the core the record path shares), after the header initialisation, `AnimFlags` is the byte `0xD` of the animation set of the target animation (the value the binary's first switch loads, which `SyncAnimation` reloads identically when it validates the pending switch at the end of the first frame). `CurrentAnimationId`, the directions and `SpawnAnimationActive` stay as the core leaves them: the switch is pending in the four directions. `AlundraScriptedMotion.TickScriptedNpc` takes the key of its animation set on the target when the entity carries the marker and its current animation is still `~0` (the one line the generic kinematics gets, guarded by the marker); an arrow facing right then moves 3 px at its birth tick and 6 px per tick (153, 159, 165 from 150 px), as the annexe of E20.e0 gives for the binary.
- **A test seam in the shot stub** (`AlundraPlayerManager.WeaponSpawnBirthHookForTests`, per thread, null in production) calls the interface from the exact place of `MovePlayer`; nothing is spawned in production yet (the shot is E20.f3, the items E20.h3).

## Consequences

- Nothing changes in play until E20.f3 or E20.h3 call the interface: no production caller exists, and the entities from records (map load, opcodes `0x2D`, `0x8A`, `0x8B`) do not carry the marker. `Alundra.Tests`: 4 815 before, 4 833 after (18 new) in Release and in Debug; the only existing test changed is the probe `PA3` (its first case now measures the state after this slice).
- The pins of the arcs by record stay as they were (a one-frame latency for their first tick). Whether to extend the participation to them (opcodes, map events, E20.e0a4, question Q-A1 of the author) was measured with the marker forced on the record path: eight existing tests move (list in the plan, "Mesure P-B", section 1.16.4); that change was not committed.
- Known limits: the engine still integrates the entity at the next frame (it is drawn then, D-E20-35); "A twice" (D-E0A-6) and stale pointers (D-E0A-7) are not ported; the loot roll at creation is E20.e0b; the shot itself is E20.f3.
