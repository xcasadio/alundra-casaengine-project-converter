# ADR-0069: An entity born by the sprite-index interface keeps its Z in the logic tick and steps vertically before the XY step

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-10-16
- **Source**: this chantier: `docs/plan-e20-heros.md`, section 1.18 (E20.e0k) and 1.18.2 (E20.e0k2, "Résultats"); decisions D-E0K-1, D-E0K-3, D-E0K-4, D-E0K-5, D-E0K-10, Q-K1 and Q-K2 (AUTO-mode defaults); oracle `docs/plan-e20-annexe/e20-e0k/` (97-file manifest, scenarios `arrows-ball-fire`, `arrows-arrow-h1`, `arrows-arrow-h2`, `crate-flat`, `magnet-down-3px`, `magnet-down-4px`); ADR-0026 (the DLL's Z is the binary's minus one raw unit), ADR-0065 (the marker block), ADR-0068 (the map state)

## Context

- After ADR-0068 an entity born by `AlundraWorldProxy.SpawnEntityBySpriteIndex` (arrow, balls, wave, thrown bomb, dropped objects) has the map state, but its Z still went through the root of the character controller: `PushLogicalPositionToRoot` writes the root in whole pixels, and `PullRootPose` and `MoveControllerAndPullPosition` pulled `PosZ` and `IsOnGround` back from the root at every tick. The raw unit of the creation (`Z_arg - offZ + 1`) was lost at the first tick, so the Z series was the oracle minus two raw units during a fall, and an arrow flying at the height of the ground reported `IsOnGround` 1.
- The controller path of `EvaluateEntitySupport` lands with a `<=` test where the binary lands with a strict test (`PosZ + ModZ + F < T`, `0x80036C20`), applies the gravity decay inside the support block (after the XY step) where the binary steps Z before XY (`MoveEntity`, `0x80037E34`), has a 4 px ground tolerance where the binary has none outside its 3 px magnet (`0x80037848`), and lets the engine anchor the root to the ground when nothing declares the vertical as held outside.
- The hero already has this model in the air state (`RunHeroVerticalTick`, `SnapHeroAtApex`), which must not change.
- The entities placed by the map (records) have an older rule (E19.h2, paused); `EntityRefId == -1` cannot select the entities born by the interface (the adopted hero and the recycled entities have it too), and `JoinsBirthTick` is reserved for the birth tick (E20.e0a4 may extend it to records).

## Decision

- **D-E0K-1**: a dedicated marker, `AlundraEntityScriptProxy.HasLogicalZ`, posed by `SpawnEntityBySpriteIndex` only, in the marker block of ADR-0065 (the last write of the creation). Never cloned, never posed by the creations from a record, never for the hero or a recycled entity.
- **D-E0K-3**: one law of force. `ApplyVerticalForceLaw` is the only decay site, called by the support block of the controller (unmarked entities) and by the logical step (marked entities).
- **D-E0K-4**: for a marked entity `PosZ` belongs to the tick; the root follows by a vertical displacement (`FollowPosZOnRoot`), and neither `PullRootPose` nor `MoveControllerAndPullPosition` pulls `PosZ` or `IsOnGround` back (the latter by the `ZHeldByTick` guard the tick raises). The logical step is two functions called by `RunOneMotionTick` around the XY step: `RunLogicalZPreXyStep` (the law and the force of the tick, the vertical step with the strict landing, the absolute ceiling at 1 920 px, the top of the entity under the box for a subject, the sentinel) before it, and `RunLogicalZPostXyStep` (the 3 px magnet, a copy of the body of `SnapHeroAtApex`, then `IsOnGround = (PosZ + ModZ <= floor)`) after it, in the DLL's frame. `EvaluateEntitySupport` keeps the writes of `TerrainHeight` and of the floor heights for a marked entity (the binary writes them for every entity) and returns before its law and landing block.
- **D-E0K-5**: the controller's `StepHeight` is the prefab's value only with gravity and `ForceZ == 0`, otherwise 0 (the hero's S6, generalised). A marked entity declares the airborne sentinel to the controller at every logical step, again after every `Teleport` (inside `PushLogicalPositionToRoot`, which the opcodes `0x64`, `0x65` and `0x8B` call mid-life) and at every thaw (`AlundraGameplayFreeze.OwnerExternalVerticalDisplacement`), so the engine never anchors it.
- **D-E0K-10**: every pin that moved was rewritten by a derivation. One existing test moved, `AlundraMapStateWorldTests.K2` (the witness ball without a map stays at the Z it was born at, now the oracle's initial Z minus one raw unit instead of the whole pixel).
- **Measured departure from the plan's wording**: the magnet cannot be observed on the ascending side by a world test (the engine already steps 3 px up), and the oracle at 4 px above does not "not pose" (the first attempt is refused, the step is shortened by 4 raw units and the entity ends the tick on the terrain). The world test takes the descending side (`magnet-down-3px`, `magnet-down-4px`); the 3 px window itself is pinned by the direct test of the function.
- **Provisional AUTO-mode defaults, still to be confirmed or reversed by the author** (unchanged from ADR-0068): **Q-K1 option A** (only what is born during play follows the exact fall rules; what the map places keeps its older rule until E19.h2) and **Q-K2 option A** (the one-tick gap of the wall flag against the binary is accepted and documented; the full halving loop for marked entities, E20.e0k4, is built only on the author's request).

## Consequences

- An arrow, a ball, a thrown bomb or crate born by the interface now follows the binary's Z series to the raw unit (the oracle minus one), lands at the oracle's tick (11 for the ball, 23, 21 and 18 for the crate on flat, 16 px and 32 px), is not on the ground while a raw unit above the floor, keeps flying across a gel and a thaw, and is not moved by the engine between two ticks (measured gap root / logical Z: 0 raw units over 30 ticks, engine displacement 0).
- The hero's code path and the entities from a record are unchanged (no diff of `RunHeroVerticalTick`, `SnapHeroAtApex`, `AlundraPlayerManager`; the record path is guarded by the whole existing suite, 4 907 tests unmoved but K2).
- There is still no production caller of the interface (the shot is E20.f3, the items E20.h3): the change is inert in play until then. E20.f3c uses the marker as the Z rule of the flying arrow; E20.g1 sets it on an entity it lifts.
- Limits: the ceilings formed by other entities are not ported (O-E0K-6); the map edge is E20.e0k3; the wall flag still comes one tick after the arrival (Q-K2).
- Risks: a later change of the logical step or of the three guards (support block, root pull, thaw) must keep the four wirings together, since the removal of any one of them breaks the tick order or lets the engine re-anchor the entity; the world tests (z1) to (z11) and the mutants listed in the plan catch each.
