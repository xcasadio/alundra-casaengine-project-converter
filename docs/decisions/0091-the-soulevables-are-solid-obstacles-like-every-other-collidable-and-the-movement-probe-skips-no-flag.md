# ADR-0091: The soulevables are solid obstacles like every other collidable, and the movement obstacle probe skips no flag

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-10-24
- **Source**: this chantier: `docs/plan-e20-heros.md` section 1.28 (E20.g3, Q-G3-a to Q-G3-d answered by the author on 2026-10-22, defaults (A) confirmed; D-E20-27, D-E20-39); reading of `ALUN_CD.EXE` (France) in `docs/plan-e20-annexe/e20-g3/` (`bin/`, `critic/`, `critic-recon/`, `critic-complet/`); measured results in `docs/plan-e20-annexe/e20-g3/exec/README.md`. Supersedes in part D-E19-28 (ADR-0021) and D-E19-46 (decision of the author of 2026-10-03, `docs/plan-e19-opcodes.md`; ADR-0022 restates D-E19-28 in D-E19-34).

## Context

- In `ALUN_CD.EXE`, the entity-against-entity test is `FindEntityCollisionCandidate` (`0x80036F34`), called by the XY move `0x80037730`. It skips only the subject itself, takes the FIRST collidable of the list that overlaps the trial box (semi-open edges) and reads no flag of the candidate. The list (`0x800384F4`) takes an entity when `+0x6C & 0x80` (Collidable), `+0xB4 & 0x80` is clear and `+0x28 == 0` (not carried): no test of status, pickup kind, class or hero state. The entity test comes before the terrain and a refusal by an entity allows no slide. So a crate, a jar, a barrel are walls for the hero and for every collidable NPC, exactly like the iron-ball walls.
- A crate in the hands (`+0x28` set) or thrown (`0x80` cleared by the release of C23 and C70, and by C4 under conditions) leaves the list: it blocks nothing.
- Until E20.g3 the DLL passed `EntityFlags.PickupKindMask` as skipped candidate flags to `EntityCollision.FindEntityCollisionCandidate` from the only production site, `MovementObstacleProbe`: the 2,250 collidable soulevables of the export (crates, jars, barrels, trunks, stones, torches, the four objects of the sanctuary) were ghosts (D-E19-28, ADR-0021; D-E19-46, the author, 2026-10-03: "stay walkable until E14"). The reason was to leave the arcs of the story unmoved before the grab and the blows existed. They exist now (E20.g1a, g2a, g2b: `PutInHands` writes `PlatformEntity`, C23 is registered, the thrown crate is released and broken).
- The list builder (`EntitySupport.BuildCollidables`) already kept the soulevables, and the support, the ceiling, the passenger and the apex rules already counted them (the hero already jumps onto a crate).
- Measured by the DLL probes of T0 on a working copy where only the argument of the probe changed (map 392, 12 crates): stops at x 657.000 (east face) and 615.000 (west face), contact of a pile seen from the ground is the lower crate (the first of the list), a flush accepted step gives no contact (78 starts of 79 on the flush tick, 1 one tick later, like the binary), the exit of an overlap equals the binary to the third decimal, a collidable villager is stopped and a Beannoid (not collidable) crosses. No arc of the story chain moved.

## Decision

- **D-G3-1** — The soulevables (`Flags & 0x600`, all kinds including the very heavy stone of the map 4, kind 3) are obstacles for the hero and the collidable NPCs, like every other collidable entity. `MovementObstacleProbe` passes no skipped flag and `EntityCollision.FindEntityCollisionCandidate(entity, x, y, z, collidables)` no longer has the `skippedCandidateFlags` parameter (an API without caller; its test is removed with it). D-E19-28 and D-E19-46 are lifted. D-E19-34 (the native-destruction obstacles stay solid until E14) and D-E19-36 (no exit rule from an overlap) are unchanged. Q-G3-a (A): all the soulevables are solid, including the barrels, trunks, stones, torches and sanctuary objects (248 placements outside the story) that are only liftable with E20.g2c, and the seven very heavy stones of the map 4, which close paths until the glove, as in the original.
- **D-G3-2** — Q-G3-b (A): E20.g3 and E20.g1b (the lift) are merged together; between the two the crates would be walls that can only be jumped. g3 does not add a provisional lift contact (D-E20-39): the contact that g1b will read is `XCollisionEntity`, the first entity of the list that refused the step.
- **D-G3-3** — Q-G3-d (A): pushing diagonally against a crate keeps the gap already accepted for contacts (O-E19-28, D-E19-58): the controller of the engine divides the step axis by axis and the DLL slides along the crate, where the binary divides the step jointly and stops dead. Measured by P-G3-6: the hero reaches the crate face at x 657.0000 and keeps going in y (to 298.49 in 14 ticks) where the binary stays at (657.0000, 309.33). Porting the joint division is an engine plan and ADR of its own.
- **D-G3-4** — Q-G3-c (A): the fall of the crate above a lifted crate of a pile (16 px in 8 frames in the binary) keeps the old fall rule of the map objects until E19.h2; measured by E20.g4b.
- **D-G3-5** — The soulevables stay in the collidable list; a crate in the hands or thrown is out of the list by the rules that already exist (`PlatformEntity == null`, `Collidable` cleared), never skipped by the probe. The tests build both cases (`PutInHands`, the flag of `LiftableAi.ReleasedFlags`).

Gaps with the binary recorded here, not fixed by this slice (two further slices, each concerns more than the soulevables):

- **O-G3-1** — The DLL removes an entity flagged for destruction from the collidable list at once (`EntitySupport.BuildCollidables`, the filter of D-E19-29); the binary's list reads no status, so a destroyed entity stays an obstacle until the end of the physics of its tick. One tick, every entity. The premise "as in the binary" of D-E19-29 is refuted; the comment of the filter now says it is a gap. Kept: it needs a slice of its own.
- **O-G3-2** — The binary reads the animation byte (`+0xB4`) twice per tick: the list before the clock (value of the previous tick), the gate of the subject after it (value of the tick). The DLL reads `AnimFlags`, written at the end of the frame (`FrameSyncPasses`), one tick late or more. The exact equivalent exists (`HitAnimByte`, `EntityScriptProxy.HitPass.cs`). Kept: a slice of its own.
- **O-G3-3** (found by the probes of T0) — Standing on a crate and pushing against the neighbouring one, the binary gives the contact one tick AFTER the hero reached the limit (y 297.000 at t038, contact rec10 from t039); the DLL gives it on the tick that shortens the step, as on the ground (where the binary does the same, 160 runs of 160). The identity of the contact and the limit are equal. It matters only for a lift from the top of a crate on the very tick of arrival (g1b); the test pins the identity and the limit and leaves the tick out.

## Consequences

- Alundra and the collidable villagers stop against crates, jars, barrels, trunks, stones and torches, on the story chain (the map 392 barricade, the map 14 crates) and elsewhere; they must be jumped over, lifted (g1b, with g2) or broken to pass. The barricade of the map 392 needs the jump (E20.g4a) and the lift (g1b, g4b): the three are delivered together (D-G3-2).
- The slide along a crate in a diagonal push is visible on about 2,250 placements (D-G3-3); a hero or NPC born inside a soulevable stays blocked (D-E19-36); the arrivals of the portals of the story chain overlap no soulevable (checked by `portal_arrivals`, nearest gap 33 px).
- The tests (s1) to (s6) of E20.g3 and the mutants of T3 pin the rule: the mask kept for kind 1 only, kind 3 skipped, a carried or thrown crate kept in the list, a contact on a flush accepted step, the nearest contact instead of the first, a slide after an entity contact, the terrain before the entity, a collidable NPC that crosses, a Beannoid stopped, the flush edge counted as an overlap and the Z axis ignored are all killed.
- ADR-0021 and ADR-0022 stay valid for everything else (entity contacts block walks, the walk detour waits on entities, overlaps stay faithful, native-destruction obstacles stay solid): they are superseded in part, for D-E19-28 (and its restatement in D-E19-34's last sentence "only liftable entities are excluded") and D-E19-46.
