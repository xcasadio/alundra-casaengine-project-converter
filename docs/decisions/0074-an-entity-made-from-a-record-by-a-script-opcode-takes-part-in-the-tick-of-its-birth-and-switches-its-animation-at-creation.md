# ADR-0074: An entity made from a record by a script opcode takes part in the tick that sees it born and makes its first animation switch at its creation

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-10-16
- **Source**: this chantier: `docs/plan-e20-heros.md`, section 1.16.4 (E20.e0a4, D-E0A4-1 to D-E0A4-8, "Résultats" of T0, T1 and T2, Q-E0A4-1 answered by D-E20-42); the record spawner `0x8003A1B8`, the opcode handlers `0x2D` `0x8003DCC4`, `0x8A` `0x80040284`, `0x8B` `0x8004033C`, the interpreter `0x8004205C` and its two callers (`0x8003890C` the loop 2 of the programs pass, `0x8003C790` the map events) of `ALUN_CD.EXE` (France); oracle `docs/plan-e20-annexe/e20-e0a4/oracle/` (rows `REC-ME`, `REC-ME-8A`, `REC-H1`, `REC-H2`, `REC-LOAD`... of `out/rec_tick.json` and `out/rec_birth_clock.json`, `out/box_table.txt`, `out/cmp_v6.txt`); ADR-0065 (the marker, the tick of the birth), ADR-0071 (the first switch and the frame box), ADR-0026 (no floor at `0x8A`)

## Context

- The interpreter has two callers: the loop 2 of the programs pass and the map events pass. The three opcodes that make an entity from a record (`0x2D`, `0x8A`, `0x8B`) all end in `0x8003A1B8(current entity, record number, 1)`; the creation makes the first switch of the animation 0 (`0x80039EA8`, which loads `AnimFlags`) and computes the box at the position of the record (`0x80039F30`). `0x8A` and `0x8B` write only `+0x114`, `+0x118`, `+0x11C` afterwards: no floor, no new box.
- The newborn takes part in the tick that sees it born. Born in the map events pass, its program A, status 2, `+0x1C`, lists, first clock call (counted) and physics are all at S. Born in the programs of another entity (the loop 2), they are at S+1 (the program A also turns once at S with status 1 when no slot is free under the highest one, the cases `REC-H1`, "A deux fois"). The rows `REC-H1` and `REC-H2` are identical to the rows `BT-H1` and `BT-H2` of the interface; `REC-ME` is identical to `WAVE` relative to S.
- The DLL, before E20.e0a4, made these entities wait for the next frame (the engine integrates them at the head of it), with the clock one tick late and no box at the creation. The two flags that fix this were proven for the interface of the sprite index: `SwitchedAtCreation` (ADR-0071) and `JoinsBirthTick` (ADR-0065). `JoinsBirthTick` alone regresses the box of a map-event birth (the timeline frozen at null by the creation is refrozen by the physics of S before the integration), so the two go together, A then B.
- Posed before the push of the position that `0x8A`/`0x8B` write, `JoinsBirthTick` would make `ClampToGround` read the field of the host and raise a written position that is under the terrain, which the binary does not do.

## Decision

- **D-E0A4-1 (perimeter)**: the births by script only: `SpawnEntityByRecordId` for the clock and `AnimFlags`, the three opcodes for the participation. Not `ApplyRecord`, the shared factory, the loop of the map load, `AdoptPlayerPawn`, the gull (D-E20-36).
- **D-E0A4-2 (two flags, two phases)**: phase A, in `SpawnEntityByRecordId` after `ScriptHost` and before `InitializeBalance`: `SwitchedAtCreation = true` and `AnimFlags` loaded from the byte of the creation animation (D-E0A4-4). Phase B: `JoinsBirthTick = true`.
- **D-E0A4-3 (the marker is the last write of each opcode)**: `0x2D` poses it right after a non-null return of `SpawnEntityByRecordId`; `0x8A` after its `PushLogicalPositionToRoot`; `0x8B` after its push when the search finds a target and after the search when it finds none (`spawned` non-null). One private helper of the runner does the write. No signature changes (`IEntityWorldContext.SpawnEntityByRecordId` is untouched); `SpawnEntityByRecordId` itself never poses the marker, so the map load and any caller that is not an opcode keep their exact behaviour.
- **D-E0A4-5 ("A deux fois" kept, D-E20-42)**: `EventTrigger` stays -1; the program A of an entity born in the loop 2 turns once, at S+1. Pinned by a test named after the deviation.
- **D-E0A4-6**: the known deviation of E20.e0a5 (the first clock tick of a record-born entity uncounted, no box at the creation) is now pinned on an entity of the map load, which does not change.
- **D-E0A4-7 (forbidden)**: no `Current = Target`, no faked `AnimationDirection`, no change of the engine, the converter, the data, `ClampToGround` or the shared factory; no `HasLogicalZ` (D-E20-37), no `_birthSpawnsThisTick` (no birth by script inside `MovePlayer`).

## Consequences

- A birth by a map-event program turns at S: status 2 after the programs, program A once, `+0x1C` 1, in the update and hit lists, box cursor 1, 2, 3, 4 at S .. S+3, end flag at S+25, at 1, 2 and 3 ticks per frame and wherever in the frame the entity is born; a birth in the programs of another entity turns from S+1, in the same frame when the frame has another tick, end flag at S+26 (tests `AlundraRecordBirthClockTests` for the clock, `AlundraRecordBirthTickTests` for the participation, values read by key from the oracle).
- The position written by `0x8A` or `0x8B` is not raised to the ground at the creation, and the host's collision field is read only from the tick of the birth on; at the first physics tick the entity takes the speed of its target animation while its current animation is not validated (the key `~0u` of `AlundraScriptedMotion`).
- Existing tests moved (rewritten by derivation of the rule, the image numbers of the arcs are not binary): phase A `s115_rec19` and the two box pins of E20.e0a5 (now on an entity of the map load); phase B `A10` of map 165 (every frame anchored on a program of the five actors one image earlier), `A6` of the ship (the three boxes and the loop one image earlier), `s36c4_rec3`, `s115_rec19`, the marker pin of `AlundraBirthTickTests`. No test of the gull, of the map load or of the hero moved.
- Known limits: "A deux fois" (above); the map load, the hero and the gull keep the delay and the missing box (D-E20-36, O-E0A4-4, the tranche of the map load); the six native sites that call `0x8003A1B8` (C[95], C[98], C[102]) are not ported and will have to pose both flags (O-E0A4-2); the cost of `Acquire` of the resolver at each `0x2D` is unmeasured (O-E0A4-3).
- Risks: a later change that poses the marker earlier in an opcode, that poses it in `ApplyRecord` or on the load path, or that drops `SwitchedAtCreation` while keeping the marker, is caught by the cases (b3), (b4), (b4b), the pins of the gull and the removal proof of the plan; the mutants of T4 each turn a test red.
