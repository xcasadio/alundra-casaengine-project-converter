# ADR-0111: The fire tome 1 is driven by the pilot 0x80035A84 and its balls by C63, registered for the sprite type 23 alone with the numeric turn of the binary, and E3 serves the types 16 and 23

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-10-29
- **Source**: this chantier: `docs/plan-e20-heros.md` section 1.31.6 (E20.h4d2, READY at the second review of revision 2.1, 2026-10-25; D-H4D2-1 to D-H4D2-7, D-E20-55, D-E20-57; rules R-EL-1 to R-EL-6 of section 1.31, R-EL-6 revised after E20.c2b); `docs/plan-e20-annexe/e20-h4d2/` (`disc/`, `critic/`, `t0/` to `t3/`); ADR-0105 (the start, the states and the slope guard of the tomes), ADR-0106 (the target query), ADR-0107 (the earth tome 1 and E3), ADR-0110 (the fire tome 2, the models of this slice)

## Context

- The item `0x2F` (the fire tome 1) is driven in the executable by the pilot `0x80035A84`, cancelled by `0x80034D2C`; the fire balls it throws (sprite type 23, of the global page) run the native handler C63 (`0x8007AC60`, slot C) while they live and E3 (`0x8007EDA0`, slot E, the handler of the rocks of the earth tome 1) when they hit. Until E20.h4d2 the DLL had the start, the states `0x32` to `0x34` and the slope guard of the tomes (ADR-0105), the target query (ADR-0106), E3 for the type 16 (ADR-0107) and a named stub for each of the three item seams of `0x2F`.
- The decompilation was not the oracle: the executable (France) was read and its code replayed by two independent index-writing machines (the discovery's and the critic's); the vectors (3 270 of the pilot, 400 of the cancellation, 288 of E3, 3 524 of C63) and the 30 world keys were made from it, and the critic's verdict prevailed (22 binary facts confirmed, two P2 corrections applied). The turn of C63 is numeric, not the shortest way: the direction wanted and the direction asked are compared as numbers, so a ball whose target is across the passage from 31 to 0 turns almost a full circle (author's decision D-E20-57, the original's behaviour kept).
- The DLL has no member for the identity words of the binary (`+0x04`, `+0x27C`, the pair kept by the pilot): the author decided (D-E20-55) that the 32-bit accessors of `+0x274` are proper to the tomes, and the target is a reference.

## Decision

- A new file `Alundra/Ai/Items/FireTome1Ai.cs` holds the pilot (`Drive`), the cancellation (`Cancel`), C63 (`BallTick`) and its registration (`Register`, `Keys`) (D-H4D2-1); a new file `TomeWords.cs` holds the 32-bit accessors of `+0x274`, proper to the tomes (D-H4D2-3).
- The pilot: phase 0 (the slope guard, then nothing until the animation is `0x33`; then the column (sprite 20, direction 0) is born at the hero's X, Y and Z, the pointer is kept in `ItemSpawnedEntity` BEFORE the test of nullity, a refused birth answers 1; the phase is 1 and phase 1 runs in the same tick); phase 1 (the guard: non-zero answers 1 with no restoration; once the column is gone, that is `Destroyed` or taken off the table, never `Slot < 0` alone, the animation is `0x34`, the phase 2; otherwise when the counter read, the value before the increment, is a multiple of 8, ONE draw below 32 gives the direction of a ball, consumed whether the birth is accepted or not, and the ball (sprite 23) is born at the hero's X, Y and 16 px above his Z with the Z force `0x80000`, the wait 16 and no target); phase 2 without guard (0 while the animation is `0x34`); any other phase answers 0 and writes nothing.
- C63: while the wait counter is not zero it loses one and nothing else is done; then the Z force is -3 px per tick and the target is handled: no target asks the query of the spells (ADR-0106; the mask `ClassA`, the type the low nibble of the attack's attribute, the count being the highest slot of the entities born, `TargetQuery.StoredCount`), none found draws the wait below 9, otherwise the FIRST becomes the target (slot and reference) and the turn is made in the same tick; a target must still be the same reference at the status Normal, else it is forgotten. The turn: `d` and the direction asked `cur` as numbers, no wrap, `d` if the gap is below 4 otherwise `cur` plus or minus 4, the wait becomes 2 (D-E20-57).
- E3 serves the types 16 and 23 through one registration: `EarthTome1Ai.SpriteTypes` is `{16, 23}`; C63 is registered for the type 23 alone, in `NativeSpriteRegistry.CreateForProduction` (D-H4D2-2). The target is a reference, "same identity" being "same reference and status Normal" (D-H4D2-4, an inference: the DLL does not reuse objects).
- The cancellation `0x80034D2C`: each entity at the status Normal in the order of the slots; the column (type 20) gives the effect `(19, 2)` at its raw position (Z in the binary's frame) through `NativeEffects.CreateFireColumnBlast`, then `DestroyEntity(entity)` (no break effect); the ball (type 23) goes to the status Deactivated and loses its blocker; the others are left. The item is freed by `PlayerManager.CancelItemMode`.
- The three entries `0x2F` of the item seams of `PlayerManager` are wired in the same slice: the start is the tome start of h4a, the driver is `FireTome1Ai.Drive`, the cancellation is `FireTome1Ai.Cancel`.

## Consequences

- The fire tome 1 works in the game: the cast pose of 32 ticks, the column at the tick 34, eleven balls 8 ticks apart that rise, fall and aim at the nearest enemy, the item freed at 149; an interruption destroys the column with its effect and sends the balls to the status Deactivated; with no free slot for the column the magic is lost and the item is freed at the tick 34.
- The five sums of the keys of the production registry are derived from `FireTome1Ai.Keys`, not retyped (`NativeSpriteRegistryTests`, `LiftableAiRegistrationTests.R1`, `ChargedArrowAiTests.C2`, `WaveSwordLifeTests.V3`, `BallFlamesTests.M3`).
- Tests: 7 931 after T3 (7 930 after T2 plus one), Release and Debug without failure. 71 real mutants of the slice were applied by script and undone (`docs/plan-e20-annexe/e20-h4d2/t3/mutants.py`, results in `t3/results.md`): 69 killed, 2 declared equivalent (the column destroyed before the effect is created, the effect taking coordinates; the turn threshold `<= 4`, the equal case being the step itself). One survivor of the first run (the word `+0x278` not cleared at the birth of a ball) is now killed by a test that primes the newborn; three mutants of the plan's lists have no analogue in the DLL (see the results).
- Deferred: the sprite types 20 and 23 are placed by no static map (the 56 dynamic-type calls of the binary stay unestablished), the other elements of the tomes, the wind tome 1 (it reuses `TomeWords`), the track XA of the cast (Q-H4-1).
