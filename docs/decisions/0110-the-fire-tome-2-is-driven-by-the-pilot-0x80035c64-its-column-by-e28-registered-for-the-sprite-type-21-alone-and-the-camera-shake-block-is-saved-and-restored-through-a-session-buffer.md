# ADR-0110: The fire tome 2 is driven by the pilot 0x80035C64 and its column by E28, registered for the sprite type 21 alone, and the camera shake block is saved and restored through a session buffer

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-10-29
- **Source**: this chantier: `docs/plan-e20-heros.md` section 1.31.5 (E20.h4d1, READY at the supplementary review of 2026-10-22; D-H4D1-1 to D-H4D1-4; defaults of Q-H4D1-1 (A) and Q-H4D1-2 (A); rules R-EL-1 to R-EL-6 of section 1.31); `docs/plan-e20-annexe/e20-h4d1/` (`disc/`, `critic/`, `t0/` to `t3/`); ADR-0105 (the start, the states and the slope guard of the tomes), ADR-0107 and ADR-0109 (the earth tomes, the models of this slice)

## Context

- The item `0x30` (the fire tome 2) is driven in the executable by the pilot `0x80035C64`, cancelled by `0x80034E08`, and the column of fire it makes (sprite type 21, of the global page) runs the native handler E28 (`0x8007FB58`, slot E) at the end of its animation. Until E20.h4d1 the DLL had the start, the states `0x32` to `0x34` and the slope guard of the tomes (ADR-0105) and a named stub for each of the three item seams of `0x30`.
- The decompilation was not the oracle: the executable (France) was read and its code replayed by an index-writing machine (the faithful keys `fire2_cast_pf`, `fire2_hurt60`, `fire2_hurt20`, the preset keys, the target keys; 60 vectors of the pilot, 203 of E28), then the keys of the original run were stored (`t0/`).
- The pilot shakes the camera while the column burns: it writes the shake block (nine words: the flag, the limits and speeds in X and Y, the offsets, the reach) and, to leave the block as it found it, saves its nine words to a buffer of the binary (`0x80127138`) at the birth of the column and restores them at the end and in the cancellation, with no guard. That buffer has no member in the DLL. The author kept the defaults of Q-H4D1-1 (A: the order of the DLL, the step of the shake runs after the entities of the frame, one tick later than in the binary) and Q-H4D1-2 (A: the buffer is faithful, session scoped, and the map load does not touch it).
- The hero is the entity the interruptions of the pilot compare against (R-EL-6, revised after E20.c2b): the columns of the hero after an interruption are compared to the bench with the poke.

## Decision

- A new file `Alundra/Ai/Items/FireTome2Ai.cs` holds the pilot (`Driver`), the cancellation (`Cancel`), E28 (`ColumnEnds`) and its registration (`Register`, `Keys`) (D-H4D1-1).
- The pilot: phase 0 (the slope guard, then nothing until the animation is `0x33`, the end of the cast pose; then the column is born by `SpawnEntityBySpriteIndex` at the hero's position, direction 0, the pointer is kept in the hero-only field `ItemSpawnedEntity` BEFORE the test of nullity, a refused birth answers 1 and captures nothing, otherwise the nine words are captured before the pilot writes the block, the phase is 1 and phase 1 runs in the same tick); phase 1 (the guard: when non-zero the buffer is restored and the answer is 1; while the column lives the shake is armed from the counter `c`: `Flag = 1`, `LimitY = SpeedY = c >> 4`, `LimitX = SpeedX = c >> 6`, the offsets and the reach left alone; the tick the column is gone the buffer is restored, the animation is `0x34` and the phase 2); phase 2 without guard (0 while the animation is `0x34`, else 1); any other phase answers 0 and writes nothing. The column is gone when its entity is `Destroyed` (recycled) or taken off the table (`Slot < 0`), never on `Slot < 0` alone (D-H4D1-3). No number is drawn, no sound is played, the hero is not moved.
- E28 (registered for the type 21 alone, in `NativeSpriteRegistry.CreateForProduction`): eight draws of the generator below `0x1801`, whether the effect can be created or not, each radius being the draw plus `0x320`; the global effect 19 of the animation 2 at `(X + TabX[4k] * r, Y + TabY[4k] * r, Z + 1)` for k from 0 to 7 (the 32-direction tables read every four entries), queued on the effect stage through the named creator `NativeEffects.CreateFireColumnBlast` (D-H4D1-4); then `DestroyEntity(self, -1)`, which reads the break effect of the header.
- The cancellation `0x80034E08`: restores the nine words of the buffer with no guard, then sends every entity of the sprite type 21 at the status 2 to the status 3 and clears its blocker; nothing else is written (the item is freed by `PlayerManager.CancelItemMode`). The test is on the sprite type, not on the table index of the sprite.
- The buffer is in `CameraSway`: `Buffer` (nine integers, zero at the start), `CaptureToBuffer()`, `RestoreFromBuffer()` with no guard (an empty buffer zeroes the block, a stale one is restored as it is, the buffer is not cleared), cleared by `ResetForTests` only, never saved, kept by `OnMapLoad` (D-H4D1-2, Q-H4D1-2 A).
- The three entries `0x30` of the item seams of `PlayerManager` are wired in the same slice: the start is the tome start of h4a, the driver is `FireTome2Ai.Driver`, the cancellation is `FireTome2Ai.Cancel`.

## Consequences

- The fire tome 2 works in the game: the 32 ticks of the cast pose, the column born at the tick 34, eight explosions at the tick 154 when it ends, the block of the shake restored at 155, the item freed at 182; an interruption puts the column at the status 3, which makes its explosions at once. With no free slot for the column the magic is lost and the item is freed at the tick 34 with the hero Idle.
- The five sums of the keys of the production registry are derived from `FireTome2Ai.Keys`, not retyped (`NativeSpriteRegistryTests`, `LiftableAiRegistrationTests.R1`, `ChargedArrowAiTests.C2`, `WaveSwordLifeTests.V3`, `BallFlamesTests.M3`).
- Tests: 7828 in the suite after T2, Release and Debug without failure. 28 real mutants of the slice were applied by script and undone (`docs/plan-e20-annexe/e20-h4d1/t3/mutants.py`, results in `t3/results.md`), all killed, none survived. One mutant listed by the plan (the box of the column that stops growing before the image 18) is a property of the sprite's data, not of the code of the slice, and cannot be applied without touching the exported data; the edge pairs of `W6b` read the real data and pin that growth.
- Deferred: the other elements of the tomes, the track XA of the cast (Q-H4-1), the injury, death and exit slot of the original, and the one-tick order difference of the shake step (Q-H4D1-1, A).
