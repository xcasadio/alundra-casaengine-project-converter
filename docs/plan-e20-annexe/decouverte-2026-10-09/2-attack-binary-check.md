Every instruction below was decoded from `ALUN_CD.EXE` with capstone, using file offset = `0x800 + addr − 0x80020000`. Nothing was written to disk. Field names such as IsOnGround and HitCounter come from the report or the decompilation. Only the offsets and the logic were checked against the binary.

## Behaviour the report missed or misstated

1. **The class mask is a remap.** Built at `393A4–393BC`: `fp = ((F>>2)&1) | ((F<<2)&8) | (F&0x1000 ? 0x800 : 0)` from the attacker's `Flags` (+0x6C), then tested as `target.Flags & fp`. An attacker whose `fp` is 0 is skipped (`393C0`). The port must reproduce this bit mapping; a "same class" test is wrong.
2. **Elemental on-hit effects in `UpdateBalanceRecords`.** Unless the response is `0x80`:
   - `attr` 6 or 10 creates attached effect 4 on the target (`395F4–39624`);
   - `attr` 7 or 9 creates attached effect 5 (`39630–39660`).

   These are the fire and ice reactions, and the report does not mention them. There is also a debug-only call `jal 80082918`, gated by `0x800DC058 < 0` and `&0x800` (`39524`).
3. **Same-tick release blocks the press** (claim 5). This affects input feel and must be ported exactly.
4. **Getting hit cancels the charge.** In MovePlayer, `31C44 jal 80031A68` reads TouchingEntity +0x224 and calls `8003A374`. A return other than −1 zeroes the timer and the pointer (`31C5C/31C68`) and enters state 0x31 (0x3A in mode 4) with `+0x1CC = 0x78`. The report lists `0x80031C5C` as a "warp" path; it is the hero-damaged path.
5. **The aura can outlive a reset.** The blocked paths `31BB4` and `31BEC` zero the timer but jump to END without calling `UpdatePlayerWeaponEffect`. The aura effect stays until the next normal tick destroys it.
6. **Charge counts during SprintDash.** b0 is set for animation 04, so holding Square through a sprint dash charges.
7. **After the attack check, the jump-state tail is the same code as Idle/Moving** (`31EB0` onward): the Cross jump at `31ED0`, Triangle hold into 0x29 at `31F14`, and the airborne fall into 0x2C. This touches the sprint surface.

## Decompilation versus binary

I re-confirmed these items from the report's section 9 in the binary:
- item 1 (the record pointer, `2ED18`, `2F774`);
- item 2 (z `+0x100000`, the `*3<<10` position offset, the `(2i+0x18)&0x1F` velocity index);
- item 5 (3 poofs);
- item 6 (no-weapon lookup reads `0x80028C13` = 0).

I did not check the other section 9 items: 3 (hold-spark jitter in the decompilation) and 4 (`CheckAndTriggerTileEffect` details).
