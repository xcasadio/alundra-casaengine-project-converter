## Decisions and deviations from the plan

- D-E0D-4 is applied in `GameplayFreeze.IsFrozen` (a blocked entity is frozen like the world is: controller stopped, playback paused, still drawn) and its one call site in `WorldProxy.cs` (the loop of `GameplayFreeze.Apply`); `WorldProxy.cs` is not in the plan's scope list, the one-line change is the only place the
  freeze is applied. The hero is blocked too, as in the binary.
- D-E0D-5 is applied in A255 of the object (`PickupAi.ChestObjectLoad`: the entity is hidden while the chest is not open) and at the birth (`ChestAi.Interact`), not in `FrameSyncPasses.cs`: nothing else in the DLL writes `IsVisible`, and a general rule "a Loaded entity is not drawn" would be a wider change that no test pins.
- The journal of the stubs (`PickupAi.FanfareStubCallsForTests`) gets three tokens for the chest: "stop" (`LoadBgm(0)` at F255), "track11" (A255 releases the object), "end" (the existing token, `StopAllSound`).
- (b6) The door of the map 115 that consumes the key (`0x84 [61]`, pc 982) is not driven: the test proves what the scripts read (the key in the bag, the flag 260 raised, an entity of the map 117 destroyed by the program that waits for the flag; for the vase, the flag 659 and the sound 223 of the `0x9F [10]` program). The crate witness
  of (b7) ("Square against a crate still lifts") is covered by the existing g1b tests (`Alundra.Tests/Arcs/LiftArcTests`, `Alundra.Tests/Arcs/LiftMap72ArcTests`, `Alundra.Tests/Hero/LiftWorldTests`), which stayed green.
- The vase chest of the map 115 is reached from the tile south of it (arrival (39, 10, 0)), the arrival north of it puts the hero one tile aside (T0 (d) 7).
