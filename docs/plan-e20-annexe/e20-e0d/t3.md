## The code (`a080a6bc`)

- `NativeEffects.CreateLifeVesselEffects(..., int animation = 0)`: the one body of the vessel and the seed, parameterised by the animation (0 the vessel, 2 the seed); `NativeEffects.MagicSeedSfx = 0x36`.
- `ItemEffects.MagicSeed(world, state, hero)`: `MpMax = SetPlayerMpMax(MpMax + 1)` (cap 4), `Mp = SetPlayerMp(MpMax)`, sound `0x36` at once, then the effects on the queue with the binary's Z (`PosZ + 1`). Called by `ApplyItem` (case `0x50`, hence also by `GiveItem`, which falls to `ApplyItem`) and by the opcode `0x82`.
- `EventProgramRunner.HandleMapTriggerCommand`: `0x50` leaves the degraded range (now `0x51`, `0x52`, `0x54` to `0x56`) and calls `ItemEffects.MagicSeed(_worldContext, _gameState, _worldContext.PlayerEntity)`.
