# E20.g4b - the rules of the pilot (written at T0 (b), before the arcs)

Plan: `docs/plan-e20-heros.md` section 1.29.2 (E20.g4b), T0 (b). Measured in `README.md` (this folder); the probes that applied them are `dll-probes/G4bT0ProbeTests.cs.txt`.
These rules **complete** those of g4a (`../g4a-route/pilot-rules.md`, rules 1 and 2, the polls, the phases `down`, `south`, `west`, `down2`): everything that file says holds here. The pilot of an arc is a function of the state of the game read before each
image; it never writes the game after the start of the arc. An **image** is one `ArcRun.OneFrame(k / 50f)` of **k logic ticks** (k = 1, 2, 3); a pad word is held for the whole image, and a button pressed in an image has its edge at the **first tick** of that image.

## Facts the rules rest on

- **The poll images** are those of g4a (rule 2): the anchor image `A` (the one where B1 runs `0x11 @104`, ending at the tick 204 + k - 1) and the polls `A + floor(16 m / k)`, m >= 1. In a poll image **all directions are let go and no button is pressed**.
- **The pose of a start** ((b2) to (b6)) is made by `Arc.PlaceHero` (the fractional position and the height written on the proxy, the root and the controller, as `Rig.Place` of the probes) **in the image that follows the anchor image**, before the scenario, never during it. The ticks of a start are counted from the pose: the tick 1 is the
  first tick of the image after the pose. The first poll after the pose is the tick 16, 15, 14 (k = 1, 2, 3) and the next one 16 ticks later (32, 31, 30). A pose waits for no poll: the poll phase of a start is deterministic (a pose at the image after *a* poll, as the g4a probes do, would shift it from one run to the other).
- **The contact** is `Hero.XCollisionEntity`, read at the start of the image: it is the contact of the last tick of the previous image. The lift needs the contact of the previous tick (a Square on the tick of the first blocked step is the sword).
- The lift happens in the **first tick** of the image `F` that holds the Square (the object is carried at the end of that tick); the gesture lasts 36 ticks (`TargetAnimationId` 5, then 12, the carry stand); the walk with the object is state 7; the throw is state 9.

## Rules

1. **State read before each image**: `PosX`, `PosY`, `PosZ`, `IsOnGround`, `XCollisionEntity`, `CarriedEntity`, `TargetAnimationId`, whether the image is a poll image, the tick counted from the pose.
2. **Square on the ground** ((b1), (b2), (b3), (b5), and the throw of (b1)): the direction of the scenario (Left for (b1), (b2); Down for (b3); Up for (b5)) is held without interruption except in the poll images. The Square is pressed, with the direction still held, in **the first image `F`, not a poll image, whose
   start state has `XCollisionEntity` equal to the expected record** (the contact of the last tick of `F - 1`). If a poll image falls between the contact and `F`, the direction is let go during it (rule 2 of g4a), pushing resumes in the next image, and the rule starts again when the contact is back. After the Square the pilot lets go of every direction until the carry stand
   (`TargetAnimationId == 12`), except where the scenario goes on (b1: Left, see below).
3. **(b1), the lift route**: `down` (Down) to `PosY >= 288.0`; `left` (Left) to the contact `rec5` (rule 2: the lift, `rec5` is carried in the first tick of `F`); `gesture` (Left held, the hero does not move) to `Carried == rec5` and `TargetAnimationId == 12`; `carry` (Left, the carry walk) until
   `PosX <= 615.0` (past the west face of the column, 626; any threshold in [598 ; 654] gives the same arrival tick in the binary, README (a1)): the Square of the throw (rule 2, a non-poll image), then `after_throw` (Left) until `PosX <= 600.0` (the hero is stopped by `rec9` at 597.0 and slides to 598.5), then
   the phases `south` (Down to `PosY >= 343.5`), `west` (Left to `PosX <= 541.0`), `down2` (Down) of g4a, to the departure through the portal 0 (`WarpDirector.Instance.HasPendingArrival`, cell (22, 23)). The break of the crate (`rec5` destroyed, 24 ticks after the throw) is observed, not awaited: the crate is not an obstacle in flight and the hero is held in the throw state (20 ticks) anyway.
4. **(b2)** pose (670, 316, z 64); Left to `XCollisionEntity == rec6` (y in [311 ; 327[), Square (rule 2). **(b3)** pose (636, 296, z 80) (the top of `rec5`, standing on it); Down to `XCollisionEntity == rec10` (y limited to 297.0), Square. **(b5)** pose (657, 312, z 64); Up to `XCollisionEntity == rec5` (y = 295.0), Square.
5. **Square in the air** ((b4)): pose (720, 298, z 64); Left held (poll images excepted). **The Cross is pressed in the first non-poll image whose first tick (counted from the pose) is >= 19**: it is the tick 19 at k = 1, 2, 3 (19 is 1 modulo 2 and modulo 3). Then the Square is pressed, Left held, in the first non-poll image whose start state is **in the air
   (`IsOnGround == 0`) and `XCollisionEntity == rec10`** (the gate of the oracle `critic/w5_airlift.py:25-29`, read at the start of an image). Measured: Square at the first tick 29, 35, 34 (k = 1, 2, 3), z 91.5, 84.0, 86.5, `rec10` carried. **Why 19 and not the plan's 12**: the binary accepts any Cross tick tj in [12 ; 24]
   (README (a4)), but the pilot lets go of Left in the poll image of the flight (the tick 15-16 at k = 2, 14-16 at k = 1, 3) and a Cross at the tick 13 (k = 2) or 16 (k = 3: the image 13-15 is a poll image) leaves no image start with the contact `rec10` in the air (measured, `dll-probes/b4.txt`, `b4_sweep.txt`): the Cross must come after the first poll; the second poll (the tick 32, 31, 30) falls in the window of the contact, and the gate takes the first image start outside it.
   The common set of Cross ticks that work at the three cadences is {19, 25}; the pilot takes 19.
6. **(b6), the roll while carrying**: (b5) up to the carry stand; the pilot then waits, idle, for a poll image and **holds Left in it on purpose** (the one image where rule 2 is broken) and in the following images (rule 2 holds again for the next polls); a roll (`0x10 @118`) is then expected, no stop; the arc ends 12 ticks after the roll. The expected: `rec5` is still carried
   (`CarriedEntity`), its height is the hero's + 32, `Rolls == 1`. A roll in any other arc is a stop of the arc.
7. **The roll count**: every arc but (b6) ends with `Rolls == 0` and the polls B1 made equal the polls the rules predict (`AssertThePollsWerePredicted`, as g4a).
8. **The slides** are not a rule: they are pinned by name per cadence (README (d)).
