## The equivalent: "the slope read after the portal" (`e01_slope_read_after_the_portal`)

Mutant: the hurt block reads `player.Slope_18c` at its call instead of `slopeAtHead` (the value retained at the head of `MovePlayer`, before `CheckAndExecuteWarp`). Result: **survived, 354 of 354 green** (`mutants/e01_slope_read_after_the_portal.red` empty), `git diff` empty after.

Reason (the rule of the equivalents of T5; no execution can tell the two apart): between the read at the head of `MovePlayer` and the hurt block nothing writes `Slope_18c` (`+0x18C`). In the binary the only writer is the ground-slope probe of the physics pass (critic A9 of the discovery; the warp, the entry and the hurt block do not touch it); in the DLL the only writer is `UpdateGroundSlope`, run by the physics pass, never inside `MovePlayer`. The two reads therefore always give the same value. The read at the head stays a rule of fidelity to the order of the binary (D-C2B-4), without a mutant. It is excluded from "all killed".
