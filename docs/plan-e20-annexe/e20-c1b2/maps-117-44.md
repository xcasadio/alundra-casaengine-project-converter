## 1. Verified conventions

- **Flags** (`AlundraGameState.cs:313-325`, `:34`): bank by bit 0x8000; word `(id>>5)&0x3FF`, bit `id&31`. Operands are 16-bit little endian.
- **Positions** (`EntityRecordMapper.cs:180, 188, 201`):
  - `PosX = (XPos*12+12) px`, `PosY = (YPos*8+8) px`, initial `PosZ = Height*8 px`.
  - Then `PosZ -= ModZ` (`AlundraEntitySpawnFactory.cs:671`) and raise to terrain (`:676-683`). Cell `Height*16 px` (`AlundraCellsCollisionField.cs:405`). Slope kind = `Slope&3`; all cells used here are 0 (flat).
  - Body = Pos + (OffsetX/Y/Z), size `(n<<16)-1` (`SetEntityDimensions`, `:715-730`).
  - Cell hero walkability: `(Walkability | GroundProperty<<8) & 0x41 == 0` (hero has ClassB, `AlundraCellsCollisionField.cs:259-296`). In these maps Walkability 1 or 65 is wall.
- **Directions** (`AnimationTables.cs:20`): `0x3A v` sets TD = {0 south, 16 north, 8 west, 24 east}[v&3]. Record `SpriteDirection&3` uses the same table.
- **Tick order** (`AlundraWorldProxy.cs:2655-2729`):
  - map events, MovePlayer, programs (record order), counters pass (`:2741`, clears `TouchingEntity` and `HitCounter`), lists, animation clock, physics, hit pass.
  - Pick (`AlundraEntityScriptProxy.cs:1519-1608`): `TouchingEntity != null` runs D instead of C that tick. So D runs at **N+1**, and C resumes at **N+2** with its pre-D state.
- **`0x37 Wait n`** (`AlundraEventProgramRunner.cs:2601`, binary 0x8003E3DC identical): the first call at tick t suspends, and it advances at **t+n+1**.
- **`0x1C 01`** (`:2807`): the first call suspends. For a Hold animation of T ticks set at tick s, the clock raises the end at tick s+T and the program sees it at **s+T+1**. Pinned by `AlundraAnimationClockDriveTests.cs:267-291` ("counted one tick after it ends").
- **`0x00 Break`** yields; the pc advances by 1 (`:411-418`). `Parameters[1]` is zeroed after every completed opcode (`:413, :467`), so equal Walk operands never alias.
- **Walk speed** (`AlundraScriptedMotion.cs:457-482`, `AnimationTables.cs:67-84`): sprite 72 anim 2 Speed 128 gives **1.5 px/tick east or west** (768*128 = 98304) and **1 px/tick north or south** (512*128).
- **Hit timing**: the sword swing is anim 0x10 (21 ticks, attribute 129 = nibble 1). The pass reads the box at BoxTicks 0 on the press tick (`AlundraFrameBoxTimeline.cs:106-125`). Cooldown: `FrameCollisionTickCounter = 25` (`AlundraHitPass.cs:147`).
- **Reach model**: my integer model of `AlundraHitPass.AxisOverlaps` (`:54-61`) and the D6 timeline reproduces the oracle reach sweep exactly (`reach-sweep.txt`). Dx -9..38 gives tick 1, dx 39..59 gives tick 5, and the dy windows at dx 20/40/55 are [-21,24], [-13,22], [-13,14].
