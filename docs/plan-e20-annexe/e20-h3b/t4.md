## The two survivors, and what they are

The plan lists "limite à 4 ; une bombe détruite non comptée ; ... étincelles à 64". Two literal readings of the list survive; neither is a gap of the tests, each is an **equivalent mutant**, and each was replaced by the neighbouring mutant that does change the behaviour (which is killed):

1. **M02** `entity.Status != EntityStatus.Destroyed` in the count of the limit. Equivalent: `EntityStatus.Destroyed` (0) is the free slot, and `WorldProxy` takes an entity out of `SpawnedEntities` the moment it sets that status (the recycling, `WorldProxy.cs` around the line 3307), so the list the count walks never holds a status 0. What the binary counts "until the recycling of its slot" is the bomb flagged to be destroyed (`FlagToDestroy`). **M02b** (`!= FlagToDestroy`) is killed by `BombHandsTests.H1_Start_TheLimitIsThreeEntitiesOfTheType2_WhateverTheirStatus_ADestroyedBombCountsUntilItsSlotIsRecycled_OtherTypesDoNot`.
2. **M04** `SparkWindow = 0x40` (64) instead of 0x3C (60). Equivalent: a spark is made while `fuse < window && (fuse & 7) == 0`, and no multiple of 8 lies in `[60, 64)` (56 is under both and 64 is not under 64), so both windows make the same seven sparks at the fuses 56, 48, 40, 32, 24, 16 and 8 (the fuse 0 returns before; a negative fuse never ticks, the status is 3 from the fuse 0). **M04b** (`0x44`, 68, which adds the fuse 64) is killed by `W1_` and `H4_C62_ASparkIsMadeWhenTheFuseIsUnder60AndAMultipleOf8_...`.

## The mutant of the plan with no direct form

"`+0x274` posé avant la naissance": in this code the fuse is a field of the bomb entity, which does not exist before the birth; the write cannot be moved before it. The nearest observable faults are covered instead: the fuse written on the wrong entity (M14, 25 red), the fuse of 239 (M03, 8 red), the null birth with a sound (M15) and the birth tick (M17, 6 red).
