## Rules of the adapter (critique section 5, point 1; plan T0 (b))

1. **A pointer of an entity <-> its `Slot`.** `+0x278` (the target kept by the ball), `[0x8012711C]` (the column kept by the driver) and the elements of the output arrays of the query are pointers; the adapter maps a pointer to the entity of the slot it points at (`(ptr - 0x80127D30) / 0x294`), and a null pointer to "none".
2. **An identity word read different from the identity stored is a DEAD REFERENCE.** `+0x04` of an entity, `+0x27C` of the ball, `[0x80127120]` of the driver and `[ptr + 4]`: the DLL has no member for any of them (D-H4D2-4: the target of the ball is a reference of its own, `SpellTarget`, the column is `ItemSpawnedEntity`). When the identity read at `[ptr + 4]` differs from the stored one, the adapter
   gives the DLL a **dead reference**: a proxy with `Status = Destroyed` and `Slot = -1`; when it is equal, the live entity of the slot. The test `Status == Normal` (the ball's rule "status 2 and same identity") is read on that proxy.
3. **The Z.** The DLL's Z is the binary's minus 1 (ADR-0026): the adapter posts `PosZ = word - 1` for every `+0x11C` it reads (the hero's, the column's, the ball's), and compares `word - 1` for the positions written (the birth, the effect `(19, 2, X, Y, Z)` of the cancellation: the DLL passes `PosZ + 1`, the binary's frame).
4. **The `binary_only` vectors** (87 of the driver, 81 of the cancellation: a non-zero `+0x1B8` or `+0x28`) are skipped by their flag: comparable 3 183 + 319 + 288 + 3 524. Observation at the HEAD: since E20.g1a W5 the one-argument `WorldProxy.DestroyEntity(e)` clears the carrier (`PlatformEntity` -> `CarriedEntity`, `+0x28` -> `+0x2C`), so the vectors whose only non-zero word is a `+0x28`
   (37 of the driver, 23 of the cancellation, counted by this T0) are no longer outside the DLL; the `+0x1B8` (the attached active effect, `0x8003A5D0`) still has no member (`WorldProxy.cs:3373`, named stub E20.e). The rule of the plan is kept (skip by the flag); the 60 vectors are a possible extension of T1, not a change of this T0.
5. **The output arrays of the query** (`0x800CC058..` the pointers, `0x800CC158..` the keys; written by 660 vectors of C63) are ignored: the adapter compares the effect of the query on `+0x278`, `+0x27C`, `+0x274` and `+0x8C`.
6. **The executable's own tables** (the 32-direction and arctangent tables read at `0x80022488..0x800226D8` by `GetDirectionToTarget`, the balance bytes at `0x801E70xx` and `0x801E7800`, the jump-table words `0x8002341C`, `0x8002342C`) are state of the image, not of the entity: the adapter does not map them; the DLL's counterparts are `ScriptHelper.GetDirectionToTarget` (P15: equal on the 1 024 pairs),
   `EntityScriptProxy.BalanceRecord` (the byte `2 + type`, `TargetQuery`) and `PlayerManager.CancelItemMode` (the dispatch).
7. **An entity word with no member outside these rules** is named and stops the slice: none (section "Verdict").

## Verdict of the table

Every word read or written by the vectors has a member of the DLL, or is carried by a rule of the adapter (the pointers, the identities, the Z, the output arrays, the tables of the image), or by a member that already exists at the HEAD (`ItemSpawnedEntity`, `CreateFireColumnBlast`); the only entity word with no member is the
`+0x1B8` (the attached active effect), which is the reason of the `binary_only` flag, skipped by the plan's rule. **No word without a member outside the rules of T0 (b): no stop of this rule.** The words to create in T1 are those the plan lists: the 32-bit helpers of `+0x274`, the `SpellTarget` reference of the ball, the function of the count of `TargetQuery` (not present at the HEAD:
`TargetQuery` takes the count as a parameter and h4e1 is not done; the probes P13 compute it in the test).
