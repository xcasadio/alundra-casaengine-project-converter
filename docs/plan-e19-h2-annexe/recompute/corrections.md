## 2. Corrections to apply

Severity: **M** = misleading statement (fix), **L** = label or provenance (retag), **N** = nicety.

**C1 (L) - `values.md` header, "all MEASURED ... nothing inferred in this file".** Not true for every row. Retag in place: section 0, "update k = pass of tick s+k-1" and the `0x25` reading rule are **[R]** (0x8003B388, 0x8003DB7C); section 3, the start x 32329728, the pre-jump walk (animation 9, 159744), the steady 98304 per update and the first step 129024 are **[input]** (the first step also depends on the `UpdateAnimation` stand-in), so the PosX columns and the sweep are outputs of the real physics pass on those inputs, not measurements of the game; section 4, the walk at 65536 per tick is **[input]** and the "tick 4 in DLL order" is **[I]** arithmetic (the text already says "NOT run on the DLL"). Suggested header: "measured on the real physics code with the inputs listed in each section; [R], [input] and [I] items are tagged".

**C2 (L) - `values.md` section 3 (last paragraph) and `notes.md` section 1 table, "K = 19 for every start from 490.625 px ... up to 518 px".** Only the 1/8 grid of 490-494 px and ten coarse points (492, 493.3, 494, 496, 498, 500, 505, 510, 515, 518) were run; 520 gives K = 23. "Every start up to 518" is an interpolation and the upper edge between 518 and 520 is not located. The lower edge is confirmed analytically (`recompute.out` section 5). The upper part of the interval depends on the wall blocking of ComputeXY (0x80037730), which neither the annex's sweep reasoning nor my model explains; my free-walk model gives K < 19 from x0 = 513.1 px (the box would enter the 16 px column while still lower than the ledge top), the annex's engine gives K = 19 there (T_first = 4 at 515 px), presumably because the real ComputeXY holds the box at the wall until the entity is above 16 px **[I]**. Reword: "K = 19 at every run start between 490.625 and 518 px (10 coarse points and the fine grid), K = 23 at 520 px and below 490.6 px".

**C3 (M) - `dll-delta.md` section 2.8 (A10J) and the matrix row, "PosX after update 19 = 522.28 px, inside the pin's window".** The pin is `Assert.InRange(gilesStart.X / 65536.0, 521.5, 522.5)` on `giles[FrameOf(6394) - 1].Pose` (`AlundraHeroJumpArcTests.cs:162-163`), an **absolute** X pin of the DLL, and 522.28 is the binary-XY value, which lags the DLL's XY by 61440 units (0.9375 px) from update 2 on (the next bullet of the annex says so). With the DLL's XY unchanged (plan, `docs/plan-e19-d2c-valeurs.md:28`: 32716800 at F0+103 then +98304 per image) the pose today is 34191360 = 521.72 px (sample F0+118, `@6394` at F0+119); once `0x25 @6393` returns one image later, `@6394` starts at F0+120, the sample is F0+119 = 34289664 = **523.22 px, 0.72 px above the window**. **[I]** (prediction from plan numbers and the annex's landing tick). So this pin does not stay green by itself: it must be re-pinned (about 523.2) or widened when h2 lands, together with the relational ones. Replace the sentence by this, and replace "touches no pin of A10J that is relational +-1 px" by "touches the absolute pin `:163`".

TR-V: identical for S-TRV, R-TRV-rec69, R-TRV-rec81") and `dll-delta.md` section 2.5 ("the real bouquets rec69 and rec81 give the same table").** The R-TRV replays put the bouquet at its **record** cell (terrain 0 px; the case title in `scenarios_h2.py` says "the drop is elsewhere"). **C5 (L) - `dll-delta.md` section 2.6, column "binary, first jump [M]".** `Range(597, 21)` and `617` are the measured offsets (first poll s+3, return s+23) added to the **DLL's** frame of the `1A`, s = 594 (`:201` and the comment above it).
