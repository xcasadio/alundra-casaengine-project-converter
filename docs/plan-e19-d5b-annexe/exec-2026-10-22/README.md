# E19.d2c2 D5b: read-only discovery of 2026-10-22 (all passengers follow their carrier)

Provenance: read-only discovery of 2026-10-22 at HEAD `1d0b6953` (branch `chantier/theme1-porter-plateforme`), building on the design
study of 2026-10-17 (`../report.md`, `../check.md`) and the author's answers (D-E19-117, D-E19-124). Nothing in the repository was changed:
the DLL was measured on copies of the HEAD outside the repository (`probe0`: baseline; `probe`: the prototype), not kept here. Plan:
`docs/plan-e19-opcodes.md` §1.2h.3.2b. `SHA1SUMS.txt` lists the files.

- `report.md`: the discovery's report (French), verbatim.
- `vec_d5b_out.txt`: values of the replayed binary (E22, DESC, CRATE, NPC, PILE_Z, PILE_XY, landing on a rising platform, dock,
  teleport), generated by `annex-delta/work/vec_d5b.py`; `vec_p_*.txt`, `vec_xy_*.txt`: reruns of the annex models with new seeds.
- Censuses of the corpus: `census1.py` to `census3.py` and their JSON, `edges.py`, `agg.py`. Map dumps: `map22.py`, `map22_dump.txt`,
  `map83_dump.txt`, `map344_dump.txt`.
- Probes on the real DLL: `probe/D5bProbeTests.cs` (the probe tests, run in the copies), `final_baseline_probe_out.txt` (HEAD),
  `final_prototype_probe_out.txt` (prototype), `trxsum.py`.
- `proto/prototype.diff` (238 lines added, 17 removed, Debug only): proof of feasibility, NOT a patch to apply; `proto/*.py` the scripts
  that wrote it, `proto/p11.txt` a probe output.
- `annex-delta/`: the 3 files of the discovery's copy of `../work/` and `../check/` that differ from (or are absent in) the
  committed annex (115 files were byte-identical and are not copied). To rerun: copy `../work/` and `../check/` to a scratch folder and
  overlay `annex-delta/work/` and `annex-delta/check/`.

The SHA-1 in `SHA1SUMS.txt` are those of the working-copy files when this folder was committed (outputs written on Windows may carry CRLF
there; git stores LF with `core.autocrlf=true`): compare with line endings normalized.
