## 2. Refuted or to correct

- "The draw ... identical at 1 tick per frame" is too strong. The closing runs in `RunFrame`, AFTER all draws made at the time of the frame's scripts (pending rank 2 triggers, following map and entity programs). The binary draws at the script's position. The order therefore differs even at 1 tick per frame as soon as another script draws in the frame. No effect on the 8 sites (no `0x0C`/`0x8C` instruction in the five maps; the random-walk modes were not swept) but the ADR wording must be exact.
- Acceptance criterion 2 "seed `0xC5BF9705`": the state of `AlundraRandom` is an unmasked `ulong` (after 8 draws `0xC4D0C6F7C5BF9705`); the assertion must be `(uint)AlundraRandom.RandomSeed` (convention of the existing tests), otherwise red for the wrong reasons.
- Inconsequential detail: `GetFreeEffect` returns the slot address and not an index.
- "156 of the 204 deformed quads": the 204 references are exact; my count (aligned rectangle of exact size W x H) gives 163. Different definition, same order of magnitude.

## 3. Missing, with impact on the plan

1. `0x800333AC` has THREE callers, not one: `0x800341C0` (opcode), `0x80033E70` (inside `0x80033DBC`, ids 0x45..0x56, called at `0x8007C358`) and `0x80034080` (inside `0x80033F00`, ids < 0x57 with requirement test, called at `0x8007C598`), both from the overlay function `0x8007C174` (native chest and pickup behaviour, `a0 = 0x80127D30`). Data: 39 "Treasure chest" entities on 39 maps have `Contents == 0x53`, and the object entity `I83_Recipient de vie` is placed on 8 maps (134 x9, 163 [chain], 258, 298, 302, 398, 439, 17) of which 439 and 17 have no program at all. The 8 script sites are therefore the minority of the vases. Impact: scope statement of the plan and the ADR ("vases by script only, chests and pickups with E14", already the rule of D-E19-96 and of line 6533 of the plan), recipe without a "vase delivered" promise, pure function reusable by E14 with the hero position passed as a parameter.
2. D-E19-96 puts in G3d "the interface of the native creators (... modes 1 and 3)": the report files R4 as an optional second commit. The plan must decide explicitly (include R4, or obtain the author's agreement to postpone it and amend D-E19-96 and line 8675). R4 has no consumer in the DLL (6 native sites, all E13/E14) and the depth key `+0x1BC` has no equivalent.
3. Variant A is a provisional seam: the end-of-`MovePlayer` sync (`PlayerManager.cs:947-950`) and the entity's `Hp` are deliberately not ported (`AlundraPlayerManager.cs` ~421). The ADR must say that porting this sync requires switching to variant B at the same time, otherwise it would overwrite the stats with the entity's 0 values.
4. Recipe: one map of the five requires a new preset in `AlundraTestSaves.cs`, outside the proposed closed list. Either add it, or write "headless proof only".

## 4. Not confirmed

- That the chest and pickup vases do go through `0x8007C174` (only the call graph was read, not the `Contents` -> `+0x68` path).
- That the `I83` of maps 163, 439 and 17 are collected by native code (deduced from the absence of an `82 53` site and of programs).
- The effect of the draw order with the random-walk modes of the NPCs of the five maps (not swept).
- Nothing was executed on the DLL side (tests, game); the generator `g3d_modes13.py` was not replayed (reading of the functions and three rows checked only); the sound behaviour (anti-duplicate table `0x80165028` versus per-frame `FlushFrameSounds`) was not compared finely.
